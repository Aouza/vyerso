# Vyerso — Marketing Strategy

**Status:** STRATEGIC DIRECTION  
**Owner:** Product / Growth  
**Last updated:** 2026-10-06

## 1. Positioning
Vyerso should not be positioned as “AI reads your WhatsApp”. Generic LLMs can already comment on pasted messages.

Position around **longitudinal visibility**:
> Months or years of conversation contain patterns that are almost impossible to perceive message by message. Vyerso turns that history into something you can see and understand.

### 1.1 Brand-level thesis (wording may evolve)
Above the product positioning, Vyerso as a brand occupies a wider territory than either product: conversations carry the history of a relationship, and Vyerso turns that history into experiences to **understand** it (Nós) and **revisit** it (Nossa História). The brand must not be reduced to "discover if something changed" (that is Nós) nor to "turn your chats into a beautiful story" (that is Nossa História). The final wording is open (OD-18).

## 2. Separate product from acquisition hook
Marketing hooks can be provocative without becoming product names or predetermined conclusions.

Examples for Nós:
- “O que mudou entre vocês?”
- “Vocês sempre conversaram assim?”
- “Quem procura mais quem?”
- “Quando a dinâmica começou a mudar?”
- “Analisei anos de conversa para enxergar nossa relação inteira.”

The product remains **Nós** regardless of which hook acquired the user.

## 3. Nós acquisition psychology
Primary triggers:
- curiosity / information gap;
- validation or falsification of a perception;
- desire to see a long relationship “from outside”;
- uncertainty about change;
- curiosity about reciprocity and initiative.

Avoid marketing that guarantees betrayal, loss of interest, manipulation or a negative discovery.

### Core framing
**Nós**  
*A relação de vocês, vista pelas conversas.*

Alternative/supporting promise:
> Você viveu essa relação mensagem por mensagem. Agora veja a história inteira.

## 4. Nossa História acquisition psychology
Primary triggers:
- nostalgia;
- affection;
- memory;
- celebration;
- gift giving;
- shareability.

Potential contexts:
- dating anniversary;
- Valentine's Day / Dia dos Namorados;
- engagement/wedding;
- birthday;
- end-of-year retrospective;
- personalized gift.

Example framing:
> A história de vocês já está escrita. Ela só está espalhada por milhares de mensagens.

Nossa História can have an independent landing page and paid/organic campaigns.

## 5. Próximo Passo marketing
Do not market broadly as “save your relationship”. It is primarily a contextual offer after a user has received relevant findings in Nós.

Value proposition:
> Transforme o que você descobriu em clareza para a próxima conversa.

It should help the user prepare, reflect and communicate — not manipulate another person or guarantee an outcome.

## 6. Organic/share loop
Nossa História has stronger natural sharing potential than Nós.

Potential shareable artifacts should reveal only user-approved, privacy-safe information. Examples might include duration, message counts or timeline milestones, but exact metrics must be validated for correctness and privacy before implementation.

Sharing must be opt-in. Never expose conversation excerpts by default.

## 7. Funnel strategy
```text
Pain / curiosity traffic ──→ NÓS ──→ contextual upsells

Romantic / gift traffic ───→ NOSSA HISTÓRIA ──→ sharing
```

Do not force both acquisition streams into the same emotional messaging. Both streams may run on the same Vyerso profile; routing and metrics are in §9.

## 8. Experiments, not truths
The following require testing rather than assumption:
- TikTok vs Meta vs organic CAC;
- hook performance;
- pricing;
- Free Reveal depth;
- upsell discount;
- bundle pricing;
- gift positioning;
- share-card virality;
- audience segmentation by gender or relationship status.

## 9. Brand Home, product landings and routing
- `vyerso.com.br` is the **Brand Home**: it sells the Vyerso thesis, builds trust, presents the experiences briefly and lets someone with no defined intent choose. It does not replicate product pages. Nossa História can only be presented as available once its experience is specified (otherwise "coming soon"); do not invent features to fill it.
- `/nos` and `/nossa-historia` are **product landings**: each answers only "why should I use/buy this product?".
- **Routing:** product content → its landing directly; no-intent traffic (bio, brand search, referral, typed domain) → Home. Initial bio link: `vyerso.com.br`.
- **Navigation is not cross-sell:** the landing sells the product that brought the visitor; the ecosystem must not dilute that intent.
- **Questions this structure lets us answer** (separate metrics, see `MONETIZATION.md` §6): do visitors with no intent choose Nós or Nossa História? Which creative produces the most completed imports? Does Nossa História convert better as a direct purchase or as an upsell from Nós? In which context does a Nós buyer accept Nossa História?
